Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services.

How do I track my order?

You can track your order by logging into your account and visiting the "Orders" section in your Profile. You will also receive email and SMS updates with tracking links once your order is dispatched.

What is your return policy?

We offer a hassle-free 14-day return and exchange policy for most products. The items must be unused, unwashed, and have all original tags attached. Certain categories like innerwear and cosmetics are non-returnable due to hygiene reasons.

Do you offer cash on delivery (COD)?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available for most pin codes in India. You can select the COD option during checkout. Please note that an additional convenience fee might apply for COD orders in some cases.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery usually takes 3-5 business days depending on your location. We also offer Express Delivery in select cities, which guarantees delivery within 24-48 hours for an additional fee.
